## The mistake

Teams ask which LLM to buy before they know **which meeting** owns the metric.

## The fix

1. Pick **one** weekly operating review with a single source of truth.
2. Write the **three decisions** that meeting is allowed to make.
3. Only then automate **capture** and **routing** around those decisions.

## What we look for

- A dashboard that leadership actually opens on Monday
- Owners named on every recurring task
- Exceptions surfaced as tickets, not side chats

Automation magnifies whatever rhythm you already have. If the rhythm is chaos, you get faster chaos.
